# Portal 2 Workshop Setup Guide

## Required Software

- Portal 2 on Steam
- [SppliceCPP](https://github.com/p2r3/spplice-cpp/releases) - the Portal 2 mod launcher.
  `Setup.exe` on Windows, the `SppliceCPP` binary or `.zip` on Linux. Note that the buttons on
  [p2r3.com/spplice](https://p2r3.com/spplice/) give you Spplice 2; the package below wants
  SppliceCPP, so take it from the releases page.
- The **Randomized Testing Initiative** package by PortalRunner, which appears in SppliceCPP's
  package list once it is installed. It serves an endless queue of random Steam Workshop maps and
  filters out the ones it can prove are unsolvable. Its "Continue" feature stores your SteamID and
  up to two played maps on `epochtal.p2r3.com`; the package description spells that out before you
  agree to it.
- The [Archipelago Manual client](https://github.com/ManualForArchipelago/Manual/releases)

## Playing

1. Launch SppliceCPP, pick **Randomized Testing Initiative**, and let it start Portal 2.
2. Connect the Manual client to the room with your slot name.
3. Play the first map the queue gives you. That one is `Relaxation Vault - Test Chamber #0`, your
   warm-up. Tick it once completed, plus the three `Tutorial` checks you met along the way. Clearing `#0` always
   hands you a Clearance, which opens your first testing region.
4. Take the next map in the queue and play it.
5. Tick the challenges that map earned you, and if you finished it, tick a `Test Chamber` too.

## Check rules

**Finishing a map advances the chambers.** Tick the lowest unticked `Test Chamber #k` in any
region you have unlocked. 

**A challenge counts from any map.** Finished or abandoned, rolled for a chamber or rolled purely
to go looking, and regardless of which region the challenge is listed under. If the mechanic was
there and mattered, tick it. You have to meet requirement after unlocking the challenge, rolling extra maps to hunt one is expected (you don't have to finish those one if not specified).

**Every challenge is one-shot.** Once ticked, it stays ticked.

**You can't reroll** unless you have the item or you receive the "Force Reset" trap.

## Goal

Collect all five Diplomas to open `Final Exam`, then tick `Graduation Ceremony`.
